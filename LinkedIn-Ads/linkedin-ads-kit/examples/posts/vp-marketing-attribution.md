Attribution is hard.

Everyone in B2B marketing knows this. Dashboards, UTMs, multi-touch models, all of it. We have been debating this for years.

Here is what I have seen work:

- Talk to sales every week
- Look at the campaigns they actually remember
- Keep the story simple

Do not overthink it. The answer is usually closer than the modeling software suggests.

Marketing teams that get this right win more.
