# Form Friction Review

Use this when reviewing lead gen forms.

Read first:

- `../linkedin-ads/references/lead-form-playbook.md`
- `../linkedin-ads/references/buyer-quality-rubric.md`

## Required Context

Load brand context first, then inspect lead-form data:

- ICP and disqualifiers from `audience.md`
- offer and conversion path from `offer.md`
- latest lead form export, connected lead-form metadata, or CRM notes

If no form data is available, produce the exact export or screenshot checklist needed to review it.

## Principle

Remove friction that blocks good buyers. Add friction that filters bad-fit leads.

## Inspect

- number of fields
- business email requirement
- qualification questions
- hidden fields
- consent language
- thank-you page
- routing and sales notification

## Process

1. Identify what the form is trying to filter or learn.
2. Compare form fields to ICP disqualifiers and sales needs.
3. Decide whether the problem is too much friction, too little friction, or missing handoff context.
4. Preserve custom questions and hidden fields as sales context.

## Output

- what to keep
- what to remove
- what to add
- why this matters
- what to do next
- sales handoff note
- quality risk
