# Audience

## Ideal Customer Profile

B2B SaaS companies spending at least $25k/month across paid social and search.

## Buying Committee

- VP Marketing
- Head of Demand Generation
- Revenue Operations
- CFO

## Disqualifiers

- Students
- Agencies shopping for a client
- Companies under 25 employees

## Buyer Pain

They have plenty of leads, but sales does not trust the quality and finance cannot see which campaigns create pipeline.
