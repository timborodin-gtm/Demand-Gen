We added one question to our lead form last month and cut our "not a real buyer" rate by half.

The question was: "What is your current monthly paid social spend?"

Here is what it did:

- Students self-selected out
- Agencies shopping for a client self-selected out
- Sub-25-employee companies self-selected out
- Our sales team started every call already knowing the prospect's range

Total cost: 30 minutes of form work.
Total result: Sales accepted rate up from 18 to 31 percent in 4 weeks.

The lesson is boring: one well-placed qualifier beats a dozen retargeting tricks.

If your sales team is rolling their eyes at the lead flow, the form is probably asking for the wrong things.

Proof beats budget. Friction beats volume. Every time.
