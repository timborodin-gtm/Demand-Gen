# Lead Form Playbook

Use this when reviewing LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms, lead exports, or sales handoff quality.

## Principle

Remove friction that blocks good buyers. Add friction that filters bad-fit leads.

Low friction is not automatically good. A low-friction form that floods sales with bad-fit leads is a media problem disguised as a conversion win.

## Inspect

- number of fields
- required business email
- company size
- job title / seniority
- qualifying question
- hidden fields for campaign/ad/form/source
- consent language
- thank-you page / next step
- routing and sales notification
- CRM mapping

## Add Friction When

- sales is rejecting leads
- student/vendor/competitor leads are common
- company size is a key disqualifier
- buyer role matters
- the offer is high intent
- sales needs answers to prioritize follow-up

## Remove Friction When

- audience targeting is tight
- offer is simple and high intent
- sales can qualify quickly
- the account needs learning volume
- form answers are not changing follow-up behavior

## Sales Handoff Fields

Preserve:

- campaign name / ID
- ad ID / creative ID
- form name
- post URL for Thought Leader Ads
- buyer pain promised in the ad
- custom question answers
- submitted timestamp
- CRM stage
- disqualification reason

## Output

- form diagnosis
- quality risk
- fields to keep
- fields to remove
- fields to add
- sales handoff note
- why this matters
- what to do next
