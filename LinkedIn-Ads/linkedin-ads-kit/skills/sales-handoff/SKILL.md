# Sales Handoff

Use this when LinkedIn Ads leads need to become better sales conversations.

Read first:

- `../linkedin-ads/references/lead-form-playbook.md`
- `../linkedin-ads/references/buyer-quality-rubric.md`

## Required Context

Load brand context first:

- ICP and disqualifiers from `audience.md`
- offer and conversion path from `offer.md`
- campaign/ad/form context from the latest brief, connected cache, or exports
- lead and CRM notes when available

## Include

- campaign and offer context
- buyer pain promised in the ad
- form answers
- likely intent level
- disqualification risks
- first follow-up angle

## Process

1. Start with the buyer pain promised in the ad or post.
2. Add campaign, form, and offer context.
3. Translate form answers into a first sales move.
4. Call out disqualification risks before sales wastes time.
5. Keep the note short enough to paste into a CRM task.

## Goal

Sales should know why this person converted and what conversation the ad already started.

## Output

Include:

- why this matters
- what to do next
- first follow-up angle
- disqualification watchouts
