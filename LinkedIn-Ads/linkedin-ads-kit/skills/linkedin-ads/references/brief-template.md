```text
## Real Signal

Buyer-quality evidence worth protecting.

## Fake Signal

Metrics that look good but lack downstream buyer quality.

## Leaks

Breaks between ad, form, CRM, and sales handoff.

## Today's Moves

Specific next actions. Label each as draft-ready, manual, or watch.

## Do Not Touch Yet

Areas where the data is too thin or quality signal should be protected.

## Data Gaps

Missing exports, API access, lead fields, CRM outcomes, or sales notes.
```

The daily brief should be useful even if the user never lets the kit apply live changes.

## Deeper Operator Brief

## Header

```text
# LinkedIn Ads Brief

Brand:
Mode:
Source:
Date range:
Confidence:
Generated:
```

## Executive Summary

Answer:

- What is real?
- What is fake?
- What changed or matters now?
- What are the next 1-3 operator moves?

## Buyer-Quality Diagnosis

Include:

- qualified / disqualified / unknown signal
- CRM or sales evidence
- campaign/source patterns
- missing data caveat
- why this matters
- what to do next

## Campaign And Account Structure

Include:

- high-spend no-signal campaigns
- mixed-audience or mixed-intent issues
- budget concentration
- audience expansion concerns
- safe draft candidates vs manual-only recommendations

## Creative And Offer Angle

Include:

- strongest angle
- weakest angle
- missing proof
- cost of inaction
- offer mismatch
- rewrite direction

## Lead Form And Sales Handoff

Include:

- recognized lead fields
- missing handoff fields
- custom questions / hidden fields
- test lead flags
- form friction recommendation
- sales follow-up angle

## Thought Leader Ads Opportunities

Include:

- candidate post score
- reason to sponsor or not sponsor
- approval mechanics
- fallback Campaign Manager packet if API is blocked
- measurement notes

## Approved-Safe Next Actions

Only list machine-applyable actions that satisfy the safe apply model.

For each:

- action
- target
- reason
- risk
- rollback

## Manual-Only Recommendations

Use for:

- strategy changes
- audience review
- form changes
- sales process changes
- API-blocked Thought Leader launch steps
- anything missing current-value verification

## Missing Data

Name the next needed export, screenshot, CRM field, or API permission. Do not hide confidence limits.
