# Offer Angle Diagnoser

Use this when CTR, conversion rate, or lead quality is weak.

Read first:

- `../linkedin-ads/references/operator-thesis.md`
- `../linkedin-ads/references/buyer-quality-rubric.md`

## Required Context

Load brand context first:

- ICP and buyer pain from `audience.md`
- offer and conversion path from `offer.md`
- voice and proof from `voice-profile.md` and `profile.md`
- latest campaign, creative, lead, and CRM data when available

## Lens

LinkedIn is expensive attention. Generic product claims are usually not enough.

## Inspect

- pain clarity
- cost of inaction
- proof
- specificity
- audience language
- next-step friction
- sales usefulness

## Process

1. Identify the current promise.
2. Name the buyer pain and cost of inaction.
3. Check whether proof exists.
4. Decide whether the issue is creative wording, offer weakness, audience mismatch, or sales handoff.
5. Produce replacement angles tied to buyer pain, not generic product benefits.

## Output

Provide:

- strongest current angle
- weakest current angle
- replacement angles
- what proof is missing
- which buyer should care
- why this matters
- what to do next
