# Thought Leader Ads Rubric

Use this when evaluating posts for paid amplification.

## Thesis

Thought Leader Ads work when the post has trust the brand page cannot manufacture.

Do not sponsor a post just because it got likes. Sponsor it when it starts the exact conversation the sales team wants to have.

## Score Dimensions

| Signal | Strong Candidate |
|--------|------------------|
| ICP fit | the right roles would feel the pain immediately |
| Buyer pain | names an expensive business problem |
| Trust | sounds like lived experience, not corporate copy |
| Organic signal | the market reacted before paid spend |
| Offer fit | natural next step from post to offer |
| Sales usefulness | sales would know how to continue the conversation |

## Score Gates

- `strong_candidate`: 75+
- `test_candidate`: 55-74
- `manual_review`: below 55

## API Draft Rule

Only create a machine-applyable Thought Leader creative draft when:

- score is `test_candidate` or `strong_candidate`
- post URN is available
- campaign URN is available
- ad account is known
- permissions support the operation

For `manual_review`, produce a Campaign Manager review packet that says not to sponsor yet.

## Campaign Manager Packet

Include:

- post URL
- score and recommendation
- objective
- budget test
- audience
- placement
- UTM
- approval request copy
- measurement notes

## Measurement

Do not judge only by CPL.

Watch:

- paid engagement from target roles
- profile clicks
- lead quality
- form answers
- sales notes
- downstream opportunity creation

Scale only when the right people engage and sales has a useful conversation to continue.
