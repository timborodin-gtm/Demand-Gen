Last quarter, our CFO sat in on a pipeline review and asked one question that broke the whole meeting.

"Which campaigns actually produced the revenue we closed this quarter?"

Nobody could answer.

We had leads. We had CPL. We had CTR. We had dashboards full of charts.

We did not have proof that any of it was turning into real buyer conversations.

Here is what we learned after a 42-review audit of our own pipeline:

- 60 percent of our "qualified" leads were junk the moment a rep called
- The cheapest leads had the worst close rate (0.4 percent)
- Our most expensive campaign produced 80 percent of our Q1 revenue
- The form that converted fastest also filtered the fewest bad fits

The cost of fake pipeline is not wasted spend. It is wasted sales time, burned rep trust, and forecast numbers that nobody in the boardroom believes.

If your CFO is asking these questions and your marketing team cannot answer them clearly, you do not have a cost-per-lead problem.

You have a buyer-quality problem.

And it is the single most expensive problem in B2B paid media right now.
